# 🏢 Mess Expense Tracker App

A smart, intuitive Android application designed to simplify mess management, daily meal tracking, and monthly bill calculations. Say goodbye to manual ledger errors and complicated paper calculations!

---

## ✨ Features

* **Meal Management:** Easily track daily meals for each member with automated meal-rate calculation.
* **Expense & Deposit Tracking:** Record bazaar costs, individual member deposits, and extra mess expenses in real-time.
* **Utility Bill Split:** Automatically divide fixed monthly costs like House Rent, Electricity, WiFi, Gas, and Water bills.
* **Monthly Summary & Balance:** Instantly generate individual balance statements showing who owes or gets money back.
* **Clean & Intuitive UI:** Simple and clean user interface tailored for fast entry and hassle-free navigation.
